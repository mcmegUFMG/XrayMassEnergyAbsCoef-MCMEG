              Scientific Overview
            
            

              Extending Mass Energy-Absorption Coefficients Down to 50 eV
            


            
            

              Notice: This database is currently under development. Experimental and simulated mass attenuation coefficient data for selected materials in the sub-keV energy range will be made available following publication of the associated scientific research.
            


            

              Standard reference tabulations such as the NIST X-Ray Mass Attenuation Coefficients Database (SRD 126) provide values of the mass attenuation coefficient (μ/ρ) and the mass energy-absorption coefficient (μen/ρ) for photon energies from 1 keV to 20 MeV.
            

            

              However, in microdosimetry, low-energy X-ray brachytherapy, synchrotron radiation therapy, and nanoparticle-enhanced radiotherapy, a substantial fraction of energy deposition arises from ultrasoft photons and fluorescence cascades in the sub-kiloelectronvolt regime (50 eV to 1000 eV, or 0.05 keV to 1.0 keV). This database, developed by Lucas F. Araújo↗, Celso G. Paulo↗, and Telma C. F. Fonseca↗ — members of the Monte Carlo Modeling Expert Group (MCMEG)↗ at UFMG — provides dedicated μen/ρ evaluations obtained through Monte Carlo code simulations across the 50 eV – 1000 eV energy range for:
            

            
    
              
  • 
                Elemental Media: Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn, Pd, Ag, I, Gd, Hf, Ta, W, Pt, Au, and Bi (modeled as 100 wt% pure elements).
              
    • 
              
  • 
                ICRU 44 Tissues and Compounds: Liquid Water, Adipose Tissue, Whole Blood, Cortical Bone, Brain (Grey/White Matter), Breast Tissue, Eye Lens, Lung Tissue, Skeletal Muscle, Ovary, Testis, and Four-Component Soft Tissue.
              
    • 
            

          


          
          

            

              📂 Repository Data Structure (Easy Maintenance)
            

            

              All tabular files are stored in human-readable data/elemental/*.csv and data/tissues/*.csv formats. Currently, Gold (Au, Z=79) and Water, Liquid (H₂O) are populated with NIST SRD 126 reference data as live interactive examples while the 50–1000 eV tables are finalized for publication.
